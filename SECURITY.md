## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a prompt injection, jailbreak, or security bypass that our firewall fails to intercept, please do not open a public issue. 

Instead, please report it directly to: **hariprajwal@example.com** (replace with your real email).

We will investigate and release a patch as soon as possible. Thank you for helping make AI safer!
